# Atmos Energy

> Atmos Energy is one of the largest natural-gas-only distributors in the United States, serving residential, commercial, public-authority and industrial customers across Texas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Tennessee, Colorado, Kansas, Kentucky and Virginia. It is a regulated utility and publishes NO public developer program, API reference, machine-readable contract (OpenAPI, GraphQL, AsyncAPI), SDK, MCP server or A2A agent card. Its digital surfaces are a login-gated customer Account Center web application and a Builder Portal reached through that same login.

## Customer surfaces (web, not API)

- [Account Center login](https://www.atmosenergy.com/accountcenter/logon/login.html): Server-rendered, login-gated web application for billing, payments, usage history, e-bill, text alerts and service requests. No documented API.
- [Start service (move-in)](https://www.atmosenergy.com/accountcenter/moveininf/bpMoveInStart.html): Online start-service flow inside the Account Center.
- [Account options](https://www.atmosenergy.com/customer-service/account-options/): Overview of billing and payment options (autopay, budget billing, e-bill, mobile wallet).
- [Pay your bill](https://www.atmosenergy.com/customer-service/pay-your-bill/): Payment channels for residential and commercial customers.

## Builders and developers (construction, not software)

- [Builder and Developer Resources](https://www.atmosenergy.com/customer-service/builder-developer-resources/): The Builder Portal lets builders and property developers request and schedule natural gas service lines and meter sets. It is accessed by logging in to the Account Center; portal accounts are created by phone with Builder Services (866.332.8667) or by email (builderservices@atmosenergy.com).
- [Builder / developer request form](https://www.atmosenergy.com/form/builder-developer-request-form/): Web form for new-construction gas service requests.
- [Residential and commercial builders](https://www.atmosenergy.com/customer-service/residential-and-commercial-builders/): Builder rebate programs by service territory.
